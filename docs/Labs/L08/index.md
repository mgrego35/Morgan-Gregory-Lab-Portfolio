# L08 – Design and Print Something Useful

## Reading and Writing

Important questions are needed for design because they reveal important details. Some designs are intricate and are not simply doable by asking "what purpose will it serve?" An example of this is if you were asked to design something to hold something. You need to know what is being held, what orientation it needs to be held in, if it needs to be attached, and much more. This can only be done if you ask exact questions that reveal the answers.

## Problem Statement

There is a very common psychological concept that your space reflects your mind. My mind can sometimes feel scattered when I am at home due to my desk that always seems to be full of stuff. I noticed this when I was trying to find a certain kind of metal file for one of my prints, and could not find the correct one due to being disorganized. The target audience for this product is disorganized individuals looking to clean their desk setup without spending lots of money on drawers, filing cabinets, etc. My approach will be to create a small cup like object that is able to contain my files. There were no real strict tolerances on this since it was a simple design.
