## About Me

Hello and welcome to my portfolio. As stated in the home page, I am a 3rd year mechanical engineering major at UNCC. When I was in middle school and the beginning of high school I was always exceptionally good at math, but it was not until my sophomore year of high school that I found my passion for robotics. I joined VEX Robotics with a friend and instantly became interested in how the components come together to perform a certain task. This is when I found what I wanted to do with my future. Since then, I have continued combining experience based learning with the theory we learn in the classroom to gain a good understanding in the structural design of parts, how to choose components, and how to calculate results in order to minimize monetary loss.
